About us

Brothers from across the region

“Čuvar brata svoga” is a movement of Christian men from six countries in the region. Since 2010 we have gathered every year to grow together into the men God intended us to be in Christ Jesus.

Who we are

Men who watch over one another

We come from different towns, churches and congregations – from Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Serbia, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Slovenia. We are united by faith in Jesus Christ and the conviction that no man should walk alone.

The movement's name comes from Cain's question, “Am I my brother's keeper?” (Genesis 4:9). Our answer is: yes. We are responsible for one another – to encourage and admonish one another, to pray for one another and to stay faithful together.

The gathering happens once a year, but the brotherhood lasts longer: we take the friendships made there home with us – into our families, churches and cities.

Our purpose

Building men of God

The main purpose of ČBS is to invest in building up the men of the Body of Christ in the region. We want men to become more and more like Jesus, to live the way He taught His disciples, and to be a blessing to their families, churches and nations as fathers, husbands, sons and brothers.

“We know that we have passed out of death into life, because we love the brothers. Whoever does not love abides in death.”1 John 3:14 · the movement's key verse

What matters to us

Five things that hold us together

  1. 1

    Jesus is the only star

    We don't celebrate people or ministries. The glory belongs to Him alone – we are brothers.

  2. 2

    I am my brother's keeper

    We take responsibility for one another: we encourage, we admonish and we lift up a brother who has fallen.

  3. 3

    The Word of God

    Every gathering has a theme from Scripture that we study together through talks and workshops.

  4. 4

    Discipline

    The gathering has a light military style – rising together, line-up and physical exercise – because we believe that without discipline there is no growth.

  5. 5

    Unity

    Regardless of history, across the borders of countries, nations and churches, we show that the Body of Christ in the region is one.

What the gathering is like

Four days together, once a year

Every June we gather in a different place in the region – from Zagreb in 2010, via Lake Boračko, Adlešiči in Slovenia and Montenegro, to Dalmatia. We sleep in tents, start the day together, listen to talks, take part in workshops and spend time as brothers. Men of all generations come, and many fathers bring their sons too.
