History of the movement

Čuvari svoje braće

From the first gathering in Zagreb in 2010 to 2026 in Dalmatia – the story of men from across the region who decided to be keepers of one another.

What is ČBS

A movement, not just a conference

“Čuvar brata svoga” (My Brother's Keeper) is a Christian movement across the countries of the Balkan region. Its aim is to help men grow into the kind of men God intended them to be in Christ Jesus.

The men seek to help one another become more and more like Jesus and to live the way He left for His disciples. Once a year we gather from Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Serbia, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Slovenia to stand before God together – not only in word, but also in deed.

We show that we are determined to glorify His holy Name together, to do His will, to spread His Kingdom and to show the unity of the Body of Christ in the region.

Two guiding thoughts
1

Jesus is the only star

We are just brothers. There is no stage for people – the glory belongs to Him.

2

I am my brother's keeper

The answer to Cain's question: yes, I am responsible for my brother.

“We know that we have passed out of death into life, because we love the brothers. Whoever does not love abides in death.”1 John 3:14 · the movement's key verse
