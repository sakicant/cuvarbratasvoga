Dear brothers

What awaits you

A light military style

We believe that no army, not even Christ's, can achieve great things unless it discovers the blessing, the potential and the necessity of true discipline.

Rising together

Line-up, anthem, prayer and physical exercise every morning.

The Word and workshops

Morning and evening programme, talks and workshops on the theme of the gathering.

Life in tents

Camping in tents – simple, together, the way men do it.

Brotherhood

A prayer tent, free-time activities and fellowship late into the night.

Location and accommodation 2027

Lake Boračko

Konjic, Bosnia and Herzegovina

Lake Boračko is a mountain lake near Konjic, in the heart of Herzegovina. We already gathered here in 2015. Accommodation is in tents; if you prefer, you can arrange your own accommodation in the surrounding area. We will publish directions with the letter of invitation.
