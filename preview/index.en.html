Invitation to the brothers' gathering

Dear brothers

What awaits you

A light military style

We believe that no army, not even Christ's, can achieve great things unless it discovers the blessing, the potential and the necessity of true discipline.

Rising together

Line-up, anthem, prayer and physical exercise every morning.

The Word and workshops

Morning and evening programme, talks and workshops on the theme of the gathering.

Life in tents

Camping in tents – simple, together, the way men do it.

Brotherhood

A prayer tent, free-time activities and fellowship late into the night.

Registration fee and sign-up

What's included

Online registration is required. Payment is possible in euros only. Amounts are based on ČBS 2026 – we will confirm the final prices for 2027 with the letter of invitation.

Registration fee

80€

Taking part in the gathering, meals and a T-shirt.

Accommodation

40€

A tent at the camp, three nights. Sleeping outside the camp? You pay only the registration fee.

Deposit

20€

Non-refundable. Your registration becomes valid once the deposit is paid.

Father and son

−50%

Sons up to 18 get 50% off. The third son and every son after that – free.

  1. Sign up online

    Fill in the registration form. You will then get an e-mail with payment instructions.

  2. Pay the deposit

    €20 to the account for your country (see payments) – this confirms your registration.

  3. The rest at registration

    When you arrive, you pay the rest of the registration fee (€60) and accommodation (€40) if you sleep at the camp.

Location and accommodation

Lake Boračko

Konjic, Bosnia and Herzegovina
